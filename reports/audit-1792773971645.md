# Referral push alerts — build check, and why today's insurance count looked wrong

**Direct answer #1 — "Make sure it's been built properly, I need to know":** It has **not** been built at all yet. I went back into the real, live code just now (not relying on what was said earlier in this chat) and there is no phone alert and no Settings switch for referral requests anywhere in the app. The two referral sign-up forms (home security and insurance) still don't notify you when a client uses them. Nothing has shipped for this request yet.

**Direct answer #2 — the insurance email vs. "0 requests today":** I found a real, concrete reason this can happen, and it's not that the request vanished. The assistant chat's "what happened today" lookup checks the time something happened against the wrong clock — it's off by several hours from your local time. So a request that truly came in today (especially in the evening) can get miscounted as happening on the "wrong" day when you ask. Your email was very likely real and correctly sent; the daily count you were shown is the unreliable part. Details below.

## Findings

### 1. The referral push alert + Settings toggle still doesn't exist
- **What's happening:** I checked the two places a client actually requests a referral — the home-security sign-up and the insurance sign-up — and neither one sends you a phone alert. I also checked your Settings page's notification list and there is still no "referral requested" switch there, next to the existing ones for appointment reminders and report-ready messages.
- **Why it matters to you:** You won't get notified the moment a client asks for either service. The only way to know is to manually check the dashboard box (for home security) or your email/Sent Emails screen (for insurance), which is easy to miss on a busy day — exactly what just happened with the insurance email.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — this is the feature you already asked for; it just hasn't been built yet.
- **Where it is:** Settings → Notifications screen (`components/NotificationSettings.tsx`, `lib/notificationPrefs.ts`); the two silent sign-up forms are `app/api/secure24/opt-in/route.ts` (home security) and `app/api/insurance-referral/opt-in/route.ts` (insurance).
- **What I'd do:** Build it now — good news is the app already has a working phone-alert system used for other notifications (`lib/push.ts`), so this is a matter of wiring the two referral forms into that existing system and adding one new on/off switch, not building alerts from scratch.

### 2. Good news for your "future referral services" request — the pieces to do this right already exist
- **What's happening:** You asked that any referral service added later automatically get the same alert treatment. The app already has one shared phone-alert system that other features plug into, so the alert can be written once, generically, rather than copy-pasted per referral type.
- **Why it matters to you:** If it's built the right way the first time, adding a third or fourth referral partner later won't require rebuilding the alert — it'll just plug into the same switch and the same alert system automatically.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — really just a "build it right the first time" note, not a separate task.
- **Where it is:** Same files as above (`lib/push.ts` is the shared alert system already used elsewhere in the app).
- **What I'd do:** When building item #1, make the alert keyed off "a referral happened" in general, not off "home security" and "insurance" as two separate hard-coded cases.

### 3. The chat's "what happened today" answers can be wrong by several hours
- **What's happening:** When you ask the assistant chat things like "what referrals came in today" or "did a message go out today," it compares the exact time something happened against a clock that isn't set to your business's local time — it's checking against a time zone several hours off. That means something that actually happened today in real life (especially in the evening) can get filed under "yesterday" or "tomorrow" by mistake, and get left out of a "today" answer.
- **Why it matters to you:** This is almost certainly why you were told "0 insurance referrals today" right after you got a real email saying a client requested one. The referral itself was very likely sent correctly — the chat's count was just looking at the wrong hours of the day. This isn't losing you a referral or any money; it's just the assistant chat giving you an unreliable "today" answer, which could make you doubt something that's actually working fine.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — it undermines trust in the chat's day-by-day answers, and you're clearly relying on it to check things like this.
- **Where it is:** The assistant chat's "look up referrals" and "look up sent messages" tools (`lib/ai/askFlow.ts`, the `search_referrals` and `search_messages` sections).
- **What I'd do:** Have the builder fix those two lookups so "today" is measured using your business's actual local day (the app already knows how to do this correctly elsewhere — it just isn't used in these two spots).

### 4. There's no dashboard box for insurance referrals, unlike home security
- **What's happening:** Home security requests show up in a dedicated "Secure 24 Referral Leads" box on your dashboard. Insurance requests have no equivalent box anywhere — right now the only record you see is the email that goes out.
- **Why it matters to you:** If an email gets missed, filtered to spam, or you're just busy, there's currently no second place on your dashboard to double-check an insurance referral happened — unlike home security, which has a backup view.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — a nice parity improvement, not urgent on its own, but it would also serve as a safety net on top of fixing #3.
- **Where it is:** Owner dashboard (`app/dashboard/owner/page.tsx`) has the Secure 24 box; there is no matching "Insurance Referral Leads" box.
- **What I'd do:** Add a small "Insurance Referral Leads" box next to the existing Secure 24 one, showing the same kind of info (client, date, status).
