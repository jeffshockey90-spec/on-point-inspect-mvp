# Workflow Review — Booking → Schedule → Inspect → Report → Get Paid

I walked through the real path a job takes from the moment someone requests an
inspection to the moment you're paid and the file is closed, plus the behind-the-scenes
jobs that run on a timer (reminders, etc). The booking and report-publishing steps are
genuinely solid — duplicate-click protection, company-level security checks, automatic
"inspection confirmed" emails. The two things below are worth your attention: one is a
ticking time bomb in the automatic reminder system, and one is an old leftover tool that
could trick someone into thinking an email went out when it didn't.

## Findings

### Automatic appointment reminders could start silently skipping jobs as your job count grows
- **What's happening:** Every 15 minutes, the system pulls up to 500 inspections from your
  whole history (not just upcoming ones) to decide who needs a "tomorrow / 2 hours / 30
  minutes" reminder text or notification. It doesn't ask the database for "jobs coming up
  soon" — it grabs some batch of up to 500 total jobs (old and new all mixed together) and
  then checks each one in that batch. As long as you have fewer than 500 jobs total in the
  system ever, this works fine because everything fits in the batch. Once you cross that
  500 mark, which batch of 500 you get back isn't guaranteed to be your newest or soonest
  jobs — it could leave out upcoming appointments entirely.
- **Why it matters to you:** If this starts happening, some clients, realtors, or inspectors
  simply won't get their "see you tomorrow" or "see you in 30 minutes" text/notification —
  and nothing in the app will show an error. It would look like everything is fine while a
  client just never got reminded, which is the kind of thing that costs you a no-show or an
  annoyed client, not a bug report.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — it's not broken today if your total job count (including old,
  finished jobs) is under 500, but it's the kind of thing that fails quietly right as the
  business is busiest, so it's worth getting ahead of rather than discovering it the hard way.
- **Where it is:** The behind-the-scenes "send appointment reminders" timer job (`app/api/cron/schedule-reminders/route.ts`, around line 893).
- **What I'd do:** Have it ask the database directly for only the jobs happening in the next
  day or two, instead of grabbing an arbitrary batch of up to 500 jobs from all of history.

### An old "Email + Client Workflow" page only drafts text — it doesn't send anything, but doesn't say so clearly
- **What's happening:** There's an older tool in the app (findable through the quick-search /
  command bar as "Email Workflow") that lets you pick an inspection and an email type
  (confirmation, agreement reminder, payment reminder, report published, review request),
  and it writes you a subject + message with a "Copy" button — you're meant to paste it into
  an email yourself. This predates the real system you use today on the dashboard, where the
  "Needs Attention" list sends the actual email the moment you tap "Yes, send."
- **Why it matters to you:** Two different tools do overlapping things, and the older one
  looks and feels like it's sending something (it has email-type dropdowns, a client picker,
  a "Generate" button) when really it just writes words for you to copy. If you or an
  inspector ever use that page by mistake thinking a payment or agreement reminder actually
  went out, the client never receives anything — and there's no record of it since no real
  email was sent.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — low risk of use since it's not on the main navigation, but
  it's a loaded gun sitting in a drawer.
- **Where it is:** The "Email Workflow" page, reachable from the command bar (`app/email-workflow/page.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Either remove this old page now that the dashboard's one-tap sender covers the same ground, or clearly label it "draft only — does not send."

### The Schedule and Dispatch pages load your entire job history every time you open them
- **What's happening:** Both the main Schedule page and the Dispatch (team assignment) page
  pull every inspection you've ever created — no cutoff by date, no "just show recent ones."
  Right now, with a modest number of jobs, you won't notice. But every job you ever complete
  adds to what gets loaded fresh, every single time, by every inspector, every time the page opens.
- **Why it matters to you:** Over a year or two of steady business, these two screens — the
  ones you and your team open the most during the workday — will get noticeably slower to
  load, especially on a phone in the field with a weak signal.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — it's a slow fade, not a cliff, but it's worth
  trimming before it becomes the thing people complain about on-site.
- **Where it is:** The main Schedule calendar (`app/schedule/page.tsx`) and the Dispatch/team page (`app/dispatch/page.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Have those pages ask for a recent window (e.g., the last 90 days plus anything upcoming) by default, with a way to look further back if needed.
