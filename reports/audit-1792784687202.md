# Full scan for "generic" (non-FLOW) popups — app and desktop

I went through every screen in FLOW looking for places where a plain, unstyled popup could show up instead of FLOW's own teal-and-dark message boxes — on both the phone/app side and the desktop (regular computer browser) side. Good news: almost everything already uses FLOW's own popup system correctly. I found two new things worth knowing about — one is a real, fixable issue on the desktop side that I hadn't caught before, and the other is the same "browser's own required-field warning" issue I flagged earlier, just more complete now that I checked every form in the app.

## Findings

### On a computer, "View" on a saved company document can silently fail to open — because of a timing quirk, not a bug in the file itself
- **What's happening:** In Settings, where you keep your W-9 and other company documents (insurance, license, etc.), clicking "View" on a saved file has to first ask the server for a temporary link, then open that link in a new tab. Because there's a short wait between the click and the new tab opening, some desktop browsers (Safari especially, sometimes Chrome) don't realize the new tab is still connected to your click — so they quietly block it, the same way they'd block an unwanted ad popup. Nothing crashes and no error shows up on FLOW's side; the browser just shows its own small "popup blocked" icon up by the address bar, which looks nothing like anything FLOW would show you, and most people never notice it's there.
- **Why it matters to you:** If this happened to you, it would look like clicking "View" just did nothing — no error, no file, no explanation. That's confusing on its own, and worse if it happens while you're trying to pull up your W-9 or proof of insurance for a client, a realtor, or an insurance company on the spot.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — nobody loses data and the file itself is safe, but it's a real "button doesn't work" moment with no on-screen explanation.
- **Where it is:** Settings → your company documents section, the "View" button on a previously uploaded W-9 or company document (`app/settings/W9Section.tsx`, `app/settings/CompanyDocumentUploader.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Open the new tab the instant the button is clicked (showing a brief "loading" tab) and fill it in once the link is ready, instead of waiting for the server before opening anything — that keeps the browser from ever treating it as an unwanted popup.

### The browser's own "please fill this out" warning still shows up on a few more boxes than I first realized
- **What's happening:** This is the same issue I reported last time (the plain gray "fill out this field" bubble instead of FLOW's own styling) — I did a complete pass this time and found it also shows up on: the Reset Password and Forgot Password pages, the one-time "set up your company" form new inspectors see in Settings, and the "Title" box when adding a new report-finding template. Everywhere else in the app, including every report, inspection, and client-facing screen I checked, already uses FLOW's own warning style.
- **Why it matters to you:** Same as before — purely cosmetic, nothing is lost or broken, it just looks like a different, less-polished app for a second. The new spots I found are mostly things you and your inspectors see (not clients), except Reset/Forgot Password, which anyone — including a new inspector you've hired — could hit on their very first day.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — cosmetic only, and the pages involved are used occasionally, not every day.
- **Where it is:** Reset Password and Forgot Password pages, the first-time company setup form in Settings, and the new report-template "Title" field (`app/reset-password/page.tsx`, `app/forgot-password/page.tsx`, `app/settings/page.tsx`, `app/reports/[id]/templates/page.tsx`) — on top of the booking form and login/signup pages I flagged before.
- **What I'd do:** Same fix as before, applied to these remaining boxes, so the whole app — client-facing and inspector-facing — is consistent.

## What I checked and ruled out
- Every "are you sure?", confirmation, and type-a-name popup across reports, agreements, invoices, photos, the Coder chat, and settings — all of these go through FLOW's one shared styled popup system, on both the app and desktop. None of them fall back to a plain browser box.
- All the places that open a new browser tab or window (viewing a signed document, printing a QR code card) — only the two "View" buttons above have the timing issue; the "Print QR Card" button opens its new window immediately on click, so it's not at risk of being blocked.
- Camera, location, and notification "Allow / Don't Allow" prompts — these belong to the phone or browser itself, not FLOW, and are expected to look the way they do; nothing to fix there.
- No leftover plain "are you sure you want to leave this page?" browser warnings anywhere in the app.
