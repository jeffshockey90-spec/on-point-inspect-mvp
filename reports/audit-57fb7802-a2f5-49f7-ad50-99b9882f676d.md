## Does resending an agreement send email and text?

**Yes, both do — I was wrong earlier.** I read `app/api/send-agreement-reminder/route.ts` (used by "Remind All" / "Send Reminder" on the report itself) and `app/api/send-agreement-email/route.ts` (used by "Send / Resend" on the separate Agreements page). Both already:

1. Send the email, and
2. Also text the signer, as long as texting (Twilio) is turned on for the account **and** that contact has a phone number saved on file.

If either of those two things isn't true (texting not configured, or no phone on file for that contact), only the email goes out — and nothing on screen tells you the text didn't fire. That's true identically for both buttons; it's not a difference between them.

So there's no actual bug here — both resend paths behave the same way already. Nothing to build from this question alone.
