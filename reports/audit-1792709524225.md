# Can you see the date and time a payment was collected?

**Short answer: the system does quietly record the exact moment a payment comes in, but no screen in the app actually shows you the time of day — only the date, and only in a couple of spots. If you need the exact clock time, it's not available anywhere you can currently look.** Details below.

## Findings

### 1. No screen shows the time a payment was collected — only the date, and only in two places
- **What's happening:** When a payment is marked "Paid," the system quietly saves the exact moment (down to the second) behind the scenes. But when you go looking for it on screen, only the date shows up — never the clock time — and it only shows up on the printable invoice page and on the payment receipt that gets emailed to the client. The payment box you see on the report screen itself (where you mark something Paid, Unpaid, or Waived) doesn't show a date at all, just the dollar amounts and status.
- **Why it matters to you:** If a client or realtor ever disputes when a payment went through, or you need to double-check your own records, you can currently only confirm the day it happened — not the time. For most day-to-day use that's probably fine, but it means you can't answer "was that payment before or after 5pm" from inside the app.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — this is a quick add (show the time next to the date you already have), not a risky change, and it closes a real gap if you ever need to prove exactly when a payment landed.
- **Where it is:** The printable invoice page and the payment receipt email (`app/invoices/[id]/print/page.tsx`, `components/StandaloneInvoicePrint.tsx`, `app/api/send-receipt/route.ts`); the on-screen payment box inside a report doesn't show any date at all (`components/PaymentInvoicePanel.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Add the time of day next to the existing "Paid At" date on the invoice and receipt, and add a simple "paid on [date/time]" line to the in-report payment box so you don't have to go to the printable invoice to see it.

### 2. For payments you mark "Paid" yourself (cash, check, Zelle, etc.), the saved date is whenever you clicked the button — not necessarily the day the client actually paid you
- **What's happening:** For card payments made through the client's online payment link, the system records the real moment the charge goes through, which is accurate. But for anything you mark as paid by hand — cash, check, Zelle, and so on — the date it saves is the moment you click "Save Payment Status" in the app, not whatever day the client actually handed you the money.
- **Why it matters to you:** If you take a check on-site but don't mark it paid in the app until later that evening or the next day, the record will show the later time, not when you actually got paid. That's a minor bookkeeping gap, not something a client would ever see.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — this is really a "nice to have" so your records are exact, not something that's costing you money or causing confusion today.
- **Where it is:** The payment box inside a report, when you manually pick a payment method and click Save (`components/PaymentInvoicePanel.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Add an optional "date paid" field you can fill in yourself when marking a payment by hand, so it reflects the real day instead of whenever you got around to updating the app.
