# Does resending an agreement send it by email and text?

**Short answer: it depends on which "resend" button you use, and that's a problem on its own.**

- The **"Send Reminder" / "Remind All" button on the report itself** (the one you use day-to-day, right in the agreement area of a report) only sends an **email**. It never sends a text, even if texting is turned on.
- There's a **separate "Send / Resend" button on the Agreements page** (a different screen, not the report itself) that sends an **email, and also a text** — but only if texting (Twilio) has been turned on for your account, and only if that signer has a phone number saved on file. If either of those isn't true, no text goes out and nothing tells you it didn't.

So right now, two buttons that sound like they do the same thing actually behave differently. Details below.

## Findings

### 1. The two "resend agreement" buttons don't do the same thing
- **What's happening:** On a report itself, the button you'd normally reach for says "Send Reminder" (or "Remind All") and only emails the client — no text, ever, by design. On a separate page (Agreements), there's another button labeled "Send / Resend" that emails AND texts the client, but only if texting is turned on and a phone number is saved for that person.
- **Why it matters to you:** If you're expecting a text to go out because you used "Send / Resend" on one screen, but you're actually using "Send Reminder" on the report, no text will ever go out — and there's no message telling you one didn't happen. A client could be waiting on a text that was never going to come.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — this isn't broken, but it's confusing enough that it could cost you a client's attention at the wrong time.
- **Where it is:** Report page agreement box, "Send Reminder" button (`components/AgreementStatusPanel.tsx`) vs. the Agreements page "Send / Resend" button (`app/agreements/status/AgreementStatusResend.tsx`, `app/api/send-agreement-email/route.ts`).
- **What I'd do:** Make both buttons behave the same way (both try email + text under the same rules), or rename them so it's obvious one is "email only" and the other is "email + text."

### 2. Texting only happens if you have a phone number saved for that person AND a texting service hooked up
- **What's happening:** Even on the button that can text, it only actually sends a text if two things are both true: (1) texting has been set up for your account (this is a separate add-on, similar to how your card payments or your email sending had to be connected), and (2) that specific client or co-client has a phone number saved on their contact card for this job. If either one is missing, it just quietly skips the text — no error, no warning.
- **Why it matters to you:** If a client complains "I never got a text about my agreement," it may not be a bug — it may just be that their phone number was never entered, or that texting isn't turned on at all for your account. There's a settings screen that shows whether texting is turned on, so that part is checkable.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — it's working as designed, just easy to misread as broken.
- **Where it is:** The texting settings card in your Settings area (`components/SmsStatusCard.tsx`), and the sending logic (`lib/sms.ts`, `app/api/send-agreement-email/route.ts`).
- **What I'd do:** Nothing urgent — just know that a missing phone number or texting not being turned on is why a text wouldn't go out, and you can check texting status in Settings.
