## Does resending an agreement send email and text?

**Yes — both resend buttons send email, and also text if it's available for that signer.** There's no asymmetry here; I want to correct what I was starting to say before I'd actually verified it.

- The **"Send Reminder" / "Remind All" button on the report itself** (`/api/send-agreement-reminder`) sends an email, and also attempts a text to that signer.
- The **separate "Send / Resend" button on the Agreements status page** (`/api/send-agreement-email`) does the exact same thing — email plus an attempted text — using the same SMS logic.

The text only goes out if **both** of these are true for that signer:
1. Texting (Twilio) is turned on for your account.
2. That contact has a phone number saved on file.

If either isn't true, that signer just gets the email, and the response tells you how many texts sent, were skipped (no phone), or failed — so it's not silent on that part.

**Bottom line: no gap between the two buttons** — they're consistent. Nothing to build here unless you want to change the SMS behavior itself (e.g., always require a phone, or show the "no phone on file" skip reason somewhere on screen).
