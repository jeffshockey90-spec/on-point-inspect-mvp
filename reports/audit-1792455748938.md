# Live AI Camera — full evaluation

I went through the live AI camera's code line by line — the camera screen itself, the markup (labeling) screen, the popup system, the crash-recovery logging, and the background drafting/saving paths. The big news: I found the exact, provable reason the "popups hiding behind the camera" bug keeps coming back no matter how many times a fix gets requested. It's one wrong number in the code, and it's never actually been touched. Below that, the rest of the camera held up well — the save paths, the crash recovery, and the memory/crash safeguards are all solid, thoughtful work.

## Found it: why the popups keep hiding behind the camera

### The popup system and the camera are fighting over who's "on top," and the camera is set to always win
- **What's happening:** Every popup box in the app (the styled "Discard and close camera?" box, "Add Label," "Edit Label," etc.) is told to sit at a certain "layer" so it draws on top of everything else — think of it like a stack of transparent sheets, and each piece of the screen has a number saying which sheet it's drawn on. The popup system's sheet number is high, but the live camera's full-screen view was given an even higher number — the highest number the system allows, actually one tick higher than the popup's. So whenever the camera is open, it is physically drawn in front of the popup, every time, no exceptions. It's not a timing glitch or something that happens "sometimes" — it will happen every single time those popups are needed while the camera is open.
- **Why it matters to you:** This is the "Discard and close camera?" box someone described getting stuck behind the camera — and it's also why the "Add Label" and "Edit Label" boxes in photo markup looked broken. An inspector in the field tapping the X to close the camera with an unsaved AI write-up pending will see nothing happen — the confirmation box is there, just invisible behind the camera view — and their only way out may be force-closing the app, which could cost them the write-up they were in the middle of.
- **How urgent:** Fix now. This is a real field-use trap, not a cosmetic issue, and it's a one-line-per-spot fix once you know where to look.
- **Where it is:** The on-site AI photo/live camera tool (`components/AILiveInspectionCamera.tsx`, the camera's full-screen wrapper) and the popup system it shares with the rest of the app (`components/ConfirmProvider.tsx`). The labeling screen inherits the same problem only when it's opened from inside the live camera (`components/PhotoMarkupEditor.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Lower the camera's layer number by just one step so it's below the popup system's number instead of above it. That single change fixes the close-camera box, the Add/Edit Label boxes, and protects against the same problem anywhere else a popup is shown while the camera is open — all from one spot.

### Why past "fixes" for this looked like they didn't work
While checking, I noticed a second full-screen camera tool and a full-screen photo-viewer elsewhere in the app that were built with the exact same "set it to the highest possible layer" pattern. Neither of those currently calls up one of the styled popups, so they're not causing trouble today — but it's worth knowing this is a repeated pattern, not a one-off typo, in case it resurfaces elsewhere later. It also explains the back-and-forth: the earlier work that converted plain browser pop-ups to the styled ones (naming a service type, saving a limitation, the label boxes, etc.) was a real, separate task, and it did land correctly. But it never touched the layer numbers, so it could never have fixed the "hidden behind the camera" problem — the two tasks just got lumped together in conversation.

## Everything else checked out well

- **Saving your work is handled carefully.** Photos are saved to the phone's own photo gallery as a backup the moment they're taken, before anything else happens — so even if a save to the report fails, the photo itself isn't lost. If the AI write-up fails, the camera does not throw away your photo; it offers a "save without AI" option so the picture and your note still land in the report.
- **Crash recovery is already built in and sensible.** The camera keeps a small running log of what it was doing, and if it's ever force-closed or crashes, the next time it opens it detects that and offers a "copy crash log" button so you (or I) can see exactly what led up to it. That whole mechanism looks correctly wired up.
- **Background drafting (the beta that lets you keep shooting while AI writes up the last item) looks safe.** It writes the full-quality photo to the phone first, and only afterward tells you it's "queued" — so a background hiccup can't quietly lose a capture that you were told was safely queued.
- **The microphone and camera sharing the same hardware is handled correctly.** Dictating a note or filling in checklist boxes by voice has to temporarily let go of the camera's microphone (a known iPhone quirk — if the AI-camera microphone and dictation microphone both try to use the mic at once, the whole app can crash). The code already re-attaches the microphone before recording a video later in the same session, so a video you record after using voice input won't come out silent.
- **Nothing in this feature touches money, client-visible pages, or report wording**, so none of the liability/billing-sensitive areas apply here.

I didn't find any other correctness problems in this feature worth flagging — the one popup-layering issue above is the real, fixable item.
