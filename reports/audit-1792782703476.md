## Findings

### The public "Request an Inspection" form shows the browser's own warning bubble, not FLOW's
- **What's happening:** On the booking request form that clients/realtors fill out to ask for an inspection, several boxes (name, phone, address, city, preferred date/time) are marked as required the plain web way. If someone leaves one blank and taps the "Request Inspection" button, their phone or browser pops up its own small gray message like "Please fill out this field" — not FLOW's teal-and-dark styled message boxes.
- **Why it matters to you:** This form is the first thing a potential client or realtor interacts with — it's your digital front door. A plain gray system message breaks the polished look right at the moment someone is deciding whether to book with you, which is exactly the kind of small thing that can make an outside business look less put-together than it is.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — nothing is broken or lost, it's purely a look-and-feel mismatch, but it's on a client-facing screen so it's worth cleaning up before it's the next thing someone screenshots.
- **Where it is:** The public "Request an Inspection" booking widget (`components/BookingRequestForm.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Replace the plain browser "required field" check with FLOW's own styled warning message, the same way the rest of the app already does it.

### Login and sign-up pages have the same plain browser warning on empty fields
- **What's happening:** The email/password boxes on the Login and Sign Up pages are also marked required the plain web way, so leaving one blank and submitting shows the same generic gray browser bubble instead of a FLOW-styled message.
- **Why it matters to you:** Less of a concern than the booking form since this is mostly you and your inspectors signing in, not clients — but it's the same inconsistency, and new inspectors signing up for FLOW for the first time would see it too.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — minor, and not client-facing in the same way as the booking form.
- **Where it is:** Login and Sign Up screens (`app/login/page.tsx`, `app/signup/page.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Same fix as above, applied to these two pages, so the whole app is consistent.
