# Can you fully edit any part of a report?

**Short answer: almost everything, yes — but there's one spot that's not a type-your-own-words edit box, and one spot that's locked on purpose for legal protection.** I went through the actual screen code for the report builder (not just clicking around) to check this properly. Details below.

## Findings

### 1. The one-paragraph "Executive Summary" at the top of a report can't be hand-typed or tweaked — only AI-written or deleted
- **What's happening:** That summary paragraph you can add to the top of a report only has two buttons: "Generate Executive Summary" (AI writes the whole thing) and "Delete Summary." There is no box anywhere to type your own wording into it or edit the AI's wording afterward.
- **Why it matters to you:** If the AI writes that paragraph and gets a detail slightly off, or just doesn't sound like you, your only option is to delete it and ask the AI to write it again — you can't just fix a sentence yourself. For every other piece of writing in the report (finding write-ups, disclaimers, notes, agreement wording before it's signed), you can type and edit freely.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — it's not broken, but it's a real gap if you ever want to say something in your own words at the top of a report and the AI version doesn't match what you want to say.
- **Where it is:** The "Report Summary" box near the top of a report screen (`app/reports/[id]/AiSummaryBanner.tsx`, `components/GenerateSummaryButton.tsx`, `components/DeleteSummaryButton.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Add a simple text box so you can type or adjust that paragraph yourself instead of only being able to generate or delete it.

### 2. Everything else in a report builds and edits exactly the way you'd expect
- **What's happening:** I checked the actual edit code for findings (wording, title, severity, which section it's in, moving it to a different section, photos — adding/reordering/captioning/deleting/moving to another finding), the client and property details, report templates, section checklists, disclaimers (including your own custom wording), and the invoice/payment numbers. None of these lock up or get stuck — they stay editable at any point, before or after you publish the report or send it to the client, and the client's view updates with your edits right away (it's not a frozen snapshot).
- **Why it matters to you:** You (or any of your inspectors) can go back and fix or improve a report at any time — even after it's been published and sent — and the client/realtor will see the correction, not an outdated copy.
- **How urgent:** Nothing to do — this is confirmation, not a problem.
- **Where it is:** The report-building screen generally (`app/reports/[id]/page.tsx`, `app/reports/[id]/ReportFindingsSortable.tsx`, `components/EditableFinding.tsx`, `components/ReportDisclaimers.tsx`, `components/PaymentInvoicePanel.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Nothing — this part's working the way you'd want.

### 3. One thing locks on purpose, and that's a good thing: a signed agreement
- **What's happening:** Once a client has actually signed an agreement, its wording locks and can no longer be edited — you'd need to send a new one if something needs to change. Everything up to the point of signing is fully editable.
- **Why it matters to you:** This protects you. If the agreement text could be changed after a client already signed it, that signature wouldn't mean much if there were ever a dispute. This is working as intended, not a bug.
- **How urgent:** Nothing to do — this is confirmation, not a problem, just flagging it since you asked specifically about full editability.
- **Where it is:** The agreement section of a report (`components/AgreementBodyEditor.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Nothing — leave this locked as-is.
