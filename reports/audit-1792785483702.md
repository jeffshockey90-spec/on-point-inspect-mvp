# Checking the insurance referral logging

**Direct answer:** The email really did go out — that part is solid, it's recorded in your "Sent" email history correctly. But I found the actual bug: the lookup tool I (and Ask FLOW) use to answer "did a referral go out" is broken specifically for **insurance** referrals, so it will always come back empty for them, no matter the date. That's exactly why my first check told you "no," when the truth was "yes."

## Findings

### The referral checker is built to always miss insurance referrals, even when they go through perfectly fine
- **What's happening:** There are two kinds of referrals in FLOW — home-security ("Secure 24") and insurance-agent. They're stored in two separate lists behind the scenes, and those two lists don't have the exact same set of columns (think of it like two spreadsheets with mostly-the-same, but not identical, column headers). The tool that answers "what referrals went out" asks both lists for a column called "consent time" — but that column only exists on the home-security list. The insurance list doesn't have it. So every single time anyone (you, me, or Ask FLOW) asks about insurance referrals, that part of the question quietly fails and comes back with zero results — not because nothing was sent, but because the question itself is malformed for that list. The home-security side isn't affected; only insurance referrals are invisible this way.
- **Why it matters to you:** This isn't a one-time glitch — it's a standing bug. Every insurance referral that's ever gone out, past or future, will look like it "never happened" if you ask the chat/Ask FLOW to check. Tonight you only knew it actually went out because the insurance agent emailed you and the client called — if they hadn't, you'd have had no way to confirm it through the app, and you might have told the client "I don't see that it went out" when it actually did. That's a real trust problem with a referral partner and a client.
- **How urgent:** Fix now — this is a silent, always-on gap in something you rely on for proof that referrals happened, and it's a very small, well-understood fix.
- **Where it is:** The referral-lookup tool used by the Coder chat and Ask FLOW (`lib/ai/askFlow.ts`, the `searchReferrals` function, lines ~418-453). The insurance referral itself is correctly recorded in its own list (`insurance_referral_leads`, written by `app/api/insurance-referral/opt-in/route.ts`) and in your Sent Emails (`email_logs`) — those parts are fine.
- **What I'd do:** Have the lookup tool ask the insurance list for only the columns it actually has (drop the "consent time" column for that one list, it can just use the "created" timestamp instead, same as it already falls back to). Small, isolated fix — doesn't touch the sending or settings side at all.

### There's no dedicated screen to see insurance referrals at a glance — unlike home security, which has one
- **What's happening:** For home-security referrals, there's a whole settings panel that lists every lead that's come in. For insurance referrals, there's a settings card to turn it on and set up your agent's info, but no matching list of leads/history — the only way to check "did one go out" is by asking the chat (which, see above, has been broken for this).
- **Why it matters to you:** Even after the chat bug above is fixed, you're still relying on a chat question instead of a simple screen you can glance at. One broken piece (chat, or a future bug like this one) leaves you with zero visibility into whether insurance referrals are working.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — not urgent on its own, but it's the reason the bug above was able to hide for as long as it did.
- **Where it is:** Settings → Insurance Referral (`components/InsuranceReferralSettings.tsx`, `components/InsuranceReferralCard.tsx`) — no leads list exists here, compare to the Secure 24 leads panel (`components/Secure24LeadsPanel.tsx`, `app/api/owner/secure24-leads/route.ts`).
- **What I'd do:** Add a simple "recent insurance referrals" list to that settings card, the same way the home-security one already has.

- The actual sending logic (who it emails, what it says, that it only fires once per client per report, that it copies the referring inspector) is solid — I didn't find anything wrong there.
- The push alert that notifies the inspector when a client requests a referral (shipped earlier today) is unaffected by this — it's a separate piece of code and works correctly for both referral types.
