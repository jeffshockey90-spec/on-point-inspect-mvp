# App check-up — glitches found

I went through the app's code looking for things that are broken or likely to misbehave. I found two real problems — one that could scare a client who just paid you, and one that makes a receipt email disappear from your records even though it was actually sent. Nothing here involves money being lost or sent to the wrong place.

## Findings

### A client who just paid an invoice gets bounced to a login screen

- **What's happening:** When a client pays one of your standalone invoices online (not a full inspection payment, but the separate "Invoices" feature), Stripe sends them back to a "Payment received" confirmation page. That page doesn't require an account — it's meant to work for anyone, since the client never had to log in to pay. But the app's traffic-cop rule that decides "do you need to log in for this page" was never updated to let that page through. So instead of seeing "Thank you, payment received," the client gets redirected to the login screen.
- **Why it matters to you:** The client's card was actually charged and the invoice really is marked paid — but from their side, it looks like something went wrong. That's the kind of moment that makes someone call you worried they got double-charged, try to pay again, or leave a bad review over a payment that actually worked fine.
- **How urgent:** Fix now — this touches real client payments and creates a bad, confusing moment right after someone pays you.
- **Where it is:** The "Payment received" confirmation page after paying an invoice link (`app/invoices/payment-success/page.tsx`), blocked by the app's login-wall rules in `proxy.ts` (the `/invoices` section needs the payment-success page carved out as a no-login page, the same way the main inspection payment pages already are).
- **What I'd do:** Add this one confirmation page to the list of pages that don't require logging in, the same way the regular inspection "payment successful" page already works.

### A payment receipt email can vanish from your "Sent" history

- **What's happening:** When a client pays an inspection balance through Stripe, the app emails them a receipt and is supposed to log that in your email history so you can see it was sent. One specific piece of code that writes that log entry (used only for this Stripe payment-receipt email) accidentally tries to save the inspection's ID into the wrong storage slot in that history table — one that expects a different kind of value. That save fails quietly in the background.
- **Why it matters to you:** The receipt itself still goes out to the client just fine — this doesn't stop money from moving or stop the client from getting their receipt. But if you ever go looking in your email history to confirm "did they get their receipt," that record won't be there for Stripe-triggered receipts, which could make you think an email never went out when it actually did.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — doesn't affect money or the client experience, but it quietly breaks your own record-keeping/paper trail.
- **Where it is:** The behind-the-scenes code that runs after a Stripe payment (`app/api/stripe-webhook/route.ts`), in the small helper that logs payment-receipt emails.
- **What I'd do:** Have that helper save the inspection's ID only into the correct slot (the one every other email-logging spot in the app already uses correctly).
