# App-wide check — anything else worth flagging?

I went through the app looking for anything else worth your attention — not just the pop-up styling you already asked about. Good news first: I re-ran your app's full automatic checks myself and everything currently passes, nothing is broken. I did find one real gap worth closing (two of your "import an old report" tools don't check who's asking before doing work, which could cost you storage space/money) and one small piece of forgotten, unused clutter with your name and phone number typed into it. Nothing involving client money, client data, or report accuracy is at risk.

## Findings

### Two "import an old report" tools don't check who's asking before doing the work

- **What's happening:** On your "Import a Report" screen, you can pull in an old inspection two ways — paste a link to a Spectora report, or upload a PDF from another report-writing program. Behind the scenes, two separate helpers do that work, and unlike every other helper in that same feature, these two never check that the person asking is actually logged into your app. Anyone who finds the web address could send a PDF or a report link straight to those two helpers and skip your login screen entirely.
- **Why it matters to you:** The PDF-upload helper actually pulls the pictures out of whatever PDF it's given and saves them permanently into your app's shared photo storage, then hands back a public web link to them. A stranger could use that as a free, hidden way to dump pictures — not necessarily inspection photos, could be anything — onto storage you pay for and that lives under your account. The link-based one is lower risk (it doesn't save anything), but it still lets a stranger use your server's time to fetch and chew through someone else's report data for free, at your expense.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon. Nothing of your clients' is exposed and I see no sign anyone has actually used this, but it's an open door with no lock on a feature tied to storage you're billed for.
- **Where it is:** The "Import a Report" screen's two behind-the-scenes helpers — one reads an uploaded PDF, the other reads a pasted Spectora link (`app/api/import-report/parse/route.ts` and `app/api/import-report/spectora/route.ts`).
- **What I'd do:** Add the same "must be logged in" check that every other helper in this exact feature already has correctly (the ones that list your companies and that actually create the imported report both check this properly) — a small, consistent fix, not a redesign.

### A leftover, unused file still has your name and cell number typed into it

- **What's happening:** There's an old "send report" pop-up in the code that isn't connected to any button or screen anymore — it was replaced by a newer version. It still has your actual name, company name, and phone number typed directly into the email text it would send. Since this app is meant to be used by other inspection companies too, no screen should ever have one specific inspector's info baked in like that.
- **Why it matters to you:** Today it does nothing — it's not hooked up anywhere, so no client or other company ever sees it. It's forgotten clutter, not an active problem. But if anyone ever reconnected it without noticing, every other company's clients would get an email signed with your name and number instead of their own inspector's.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — no real-world exposure right now, just tidy it up when convenient.
- **Where it is:** An unused pop-up component (`components/SendReportModal.tsx`). The real, currently-used "send report" screen does this correctly and has no such info baked in.
- **What I'd do:** Delete the file, since nothing in the app uses it.
