# Why the Approve/Discard pop-up looks like a plain phone alert

**Short answer: you're right.** That pop-up ("Approve this change? It will go
live on your app.") is coming straight from the phone/browser's own built-in
alert box, not from any of FLOW's own styled pop-ups. The builder just never
swapped it out for one of FLOW's look-alike boxes — it's a leftover from early
in building this feature, not something that broke.

## Findings

### The Approve and Discard buttons use the phone's plain alert box, not FLOW's own

- **What's happening:** When you tap "✓ Approve" or discard a change in the
  Coder chat, the app calls a basic yes/no box built into the phone/browser
  itself — the same plain gray box you'd see from any website, with none of
  FLOW's colors, fonts, or rounded styling. Elsewhere in the app (for example,
  the "send this report" screen), FLOW already has its own nicer-looking pop-up
  it builds and styles itself — this one spot just never got switched over to
  match.
- **Why it matters to you:** It's not a bug and nothing is broken — approving
  or discarding a change still works exactly as intended. It's purely a look-
  and-feel miss: that one box breaks the illusion that you're inside a
  polished app, especially jarring right at the moment you're approving
  something that's about to go live.
- **How urgent:** Fix soon — cosmetic only, no risk to your data or your
  reports, but it's an easy, visible polish fix and you already noticed it.
- **Where it is:** The Coder chat, when you tap "✓ Approve" or discard a
  pending change (`components/CoderConsole.tsx`, lines 452 and 475).
- **What I'd do:** Swap those two plain pop-ups for a small styled confirm box
  built the same way FLOW already builds its other in-app pop-ups (like the
  "send this report" one), so it matches the rest of the app.

### This isn't just a Coder-chat thing — it's all over the app

- **What's happening:** That same plain, phone-style yes/no (and "OK" message)
  box is used in well over a hundred places across the app — deleting a
  finding, disconnecting QuickBooks, removing a team member, clearing offline
  data, and many more. FLOW does have its own nicer pop-up style built and
  used in a handful of places (like sending a report to a client), but most
  of the app still falls back to the plain built-in one.
- **Why it matters to you:** Same as above — purely cosmetic, nothing is at
  risk. But it means the "doesn't feel like it's part of the app" moment you
  noticed on Approve/Discard will keep popping up anywhere you delete,
  disconnect, or confirm something, until those are swapped over too.
- **How urgent:** Worth doing eventually — it's consistent, low-risk, and
  would take a real pass across many files to fix everywhere, so it's not a
  one-line change.
- **Where it is:** Dozens of buttons across nearly every screen in the app —
  too many to list individually, but a few examples: deleting a finding
  (`components/FindingActions.tsx`), disconnecting QuickBooks
  (`components/QuickBooksConnect.tsx`), removing a team member
  (`components/TeamManagement.tsx`).
- **What I'd do:** Treat the Approve/Discard fix above as the first of these,
  then tackle the rest as a separate, lower-priority cleanup pass whenever
  there's spare time — not something to rush.
